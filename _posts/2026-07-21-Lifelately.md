Cam and Margot asked me if I could start writing in English - so here we go:

The last weeks have been very busy, but in a very good way. The best part was having 5 days off in a row. It felt so good to have a few days to go explore and also just hang around and do nothing. 
My days off started with Savana and me heading to her families Cabin at Sheridan Lake. As we do the same job, we usually never get a day off together, so it was amazing to see her Cabin, that she had talked about a lot. It has a beautiful deck overlooking the lake. We got some wine, sat in the hot tub and chatted. Suddenly there was a beautiful rainbow and life seemed pretty good. The next day we went for a canoe ride, took the quads for a spin and went to the Iron Horse Pub. So that was a perfect start into my 5 days off and to recharge my batteries, which were quite empty at that point. The next two days were spent talking to friends, baking cookies, reading books, going for walks and having dinner with Cam and Margot. On Saturday we went to a Rodeo in Bridge Lake, which is about a 40min drive away. It was kind of exactly what you imagine a rodeo to be like, but it was great to be there and experience it in real life. And I am super happy I got to go to one! Definitely a bucket list item checked off. On my last day off Vanessa, Margot and I hiked up Mount Bowman. That’s one of the taller mountains in the marble mountain range, that we can also see from the ranch. The hike starts in a beautiful green forest, then you get to a super pretty mountain meadow before a very very steep climb to the top. At the end all of us were just panting and no one was talking anymore. Once we reached the top it was an incredible feeling and the view just stunning. I am really happy that we got to do the hike and we all enjoyed it very much. When I look at the mountain range now, I cannot believe that I was on top of one of these mountains.

After those days I had two days when everyone else was off. I start my morning without Round up and just head straight to the barn and deal with all the ponies without having to talk to anyone first. I love these quiet days and treasure them a lot. I finally had time to do some schooling with the horses and I am really making progress working with Leroy. I would say we have a solid love-hate relationship by now. I also took him on a few solo rides, which really recharged my batteries. It is just so different to spend time working with the horses and going out on a ride on my own, than  guiding guests. I actually enjoy working with the horses a lot more. It is a really rewarding feeling, when I train something with them and they get better at it. 

After a very chill week, this week is very very busy. I even had to change my normal day off and had it 3 days earlier, which means I have to work 9 days in a row. At least half of that is already over. Luckily a few of the families decided to go to the river day. So tough days in the office for me: riding down to the river, looking after the horses, reading my book, going for a swim, getting a prepared lunch and riding back in the afternoon :D 

Right now I'm simply enjoying being here, spending my summer on horseback without worrying too much about what comes next. It’s funny how it always changes so quickly. One moment we cannot stop (over)thinking everything and the next we are totally fine just enjoying the present. 

PS: While I was writing this blog post in my tent, Margot and Jack both stopped by for a quick chat. It put a smile on my face and reminded me how grateful I am for all the wonderful people I get to call my friends, both old and new.
