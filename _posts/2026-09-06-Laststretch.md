August just flew by. At the beginning we were still struggling a lot with smoky days. And honestly it sucks so much when you have to wear a mask while working outside and just have the feeling you can never take a deep breath. After those days we were busy with a lot of guests and the daily grind was just settling in. The last 10 days of August were really slow, because one big booking fell through, so we had no guests and lots of time for different ranch projects that needed to get done.

Now it's already September and 6 staff members have left already to go back to school in a few days. I can't believe that summer is almost over and on the one hand I am totally not ready to leave yet, but also a whole month seems like a long time to go. Luckily we all have a few days off right now. Unfortunately I only got 5 days and not 10 like the others. So I only went to Jack's place at East Barriere Lake with Margot and Reid and had 2 lovely days there. The lake is beautifully surrounded by mountains. And even though it was raining a lot, we had a great time. Going skinny-dipping in the rain, playing games, watching the fire, yapping and eating really good food. Saying goodbye was sad. Goodbyes are the one thing that never get easier. I know I will see all of them again and luckily Margot is also staying until the end of September, but it's the end of another chapter. And as nice as it will be to have more space in the staff house and the common areas, I know that I will miss the guys and their silly conversations. It's crazy how fast people can work their way into one's heart.

Now I'm sitting here and it feels a little lonely and quiet, because it's just Savana and me at the ranch, as we have to take care of the horses and I couldn't go anywhere else for my last days off without having a car. Sometimes my life feels surreal and I still can't believe that I just quit my job and experienced all I did this year. Seeing so much different wildlife — I even saw an American badger the other day, and a bear and her cub walking by on a ride with Leroy. I am also very much looking forward to having the whole month of October off and can't wait to travel for a little bit.

And then a new chapter will begin.
From November on I will be close to Victoria and will work with young dressage horses, starting them and training them. It will be very different from wrangling. Being a wrangler is pretty much being a people person, dealing with guests all day. Which is also fun, and I have met some very interesting people and had great conversations. But it can get a little draining at the same time. So I am looking forward to a quieter kind of horse work for a while. I am also very much looking forward to exploring the island again and seeing the ocean. Luckily Jack studies in Vic so he will still be around. It will be nice to live closer to civilization, but it will be a lot of change again. So as much as I am looking forward to this new chapter, I also know that it will come with its own challenges.

I still struggle a lot putting my thoughts into words and finding the balance of what I want to share. But anyway, I am incredibly grateful for all the people that have come into my life, and those who have been in it for a long time.

PS: Cannot recommend having to clean maggots out of a horses sheath.
